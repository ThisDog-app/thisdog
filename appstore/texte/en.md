## App Store: Beschreibung
Your dog is already at the door. ThisDog remembers your walks, shows you new paths and helps you keep an eye on how your dog is doing.

LEAD ON, TAP START, OFF YOU GO
Your phone stays in your pocket, your hands stay free for the lead and the treats. Back home, you can see how far you got and how often you've been out this week. With your Apple Watch, you can even leave your phone at home.

SOMEWHERE ELSE TOMORROW?
Your dog knows your usual walk by heart. New paths and new smells are the best thing in the world. ThisDog shows you meadows and dog parks nearby and tells you up front whether your dog can run off lead and find water there.

EVERY WALK MAKES YOUR TERRITORY BIGGER
Mark the places you like. Bit by bit, you get your own map, with the meadow your dog loves to tear around in and the dog park where the friends are.

MAYBE BUDDY IS OUT RIGHT NOW TOO
Is your dog playing with another one? Get your phones out and become friends with one tap. On your walks, you'll then see whether you're close by. Once a walk ends, the others can no longer see where you are.

WHAT IF SOMETHING ENDS UP IN YOUR DOG'S MOUTH?
Type in what it was, and you'll know straight away whether it's harmless or whether you'd better call the vet.

AND IF YOUR DOG EVER RUNS OFF
A QR code on the collar: Whoever finds your dog just points a phone camera at it and sees who to call and what your dog can't have. As soon as someone opens it, you'll hear about it.

AND TO KEEP YOUR DOG WELL IN EVERY OTHER WAY
• Vaccinations, worming, vet visits: You no longer have to remember them, ThisDog reminds you in good time.
• Partner, parents, dog sitter: Invite everyone who helps look after your dog. What one person adds, the others see.
• How much food does your dog need? ThisDog works out a good starting point.
• Won't come back, pulls on the lead or trembles at fireworks? 22 guides help you, calmly and step by step.

WHO CAN SEE YOUR DATA?
Only you and the people you invite. Appointments, health, walks and photos are encrypted so that not even we can read them. Only what's meant to be public is public: your emergency pass and place ratings.

Pay once. No subscription, no ads, with Family Sharing.

ThisDog comes from Alex in Vienna, Stella's human. The food check is a first assessment and doesn't replace your vet.
